## Critical Changes in v1.4.6

### Manifest Permission Changes

✅ **Explicitly Removed Permissions:**
- `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` (tools:node="remove")
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` (tools:node="remove")
- `android.permission.INTERNET` (tools:node="remove")
- `android.permission.WAKE_LOCK` (tools:node="remove")

### Why This Matters

**Problem:** v1.4.2 was rejected by Google Play because the build included `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` permission (added by a dependency), which contradicted our "no data collection" declaration.

**Solution:** v1.4.6 explicitly removes all network-related permissions using `tools:node="remove"` in AndroidManifest.xml, ensuring:
- 100% offline operation
- No network access possible
- Full privacy compliance
- Google Play approval
